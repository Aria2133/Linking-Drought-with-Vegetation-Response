## 1. Verified cause of the discrepancy
1. **Figure 5 is a FLUXCOM-only analysis for every GPP pair.** Its generator (`task1_figure5`, now
   `src/ecological/correlations/figure5_date_aligned_correlations.py`) keeps GPP only through 2021-12. The 16 GPP
   coefficients (GPP vs GNDVI, GOSIF/SIF, NDVI, NIRv in four land types) are identical, unrounded (< 1e-12), to the old
   Table S8 column labelled "FLUXCOM-only 2001–2021", with identical N. That column was therefore the main analysis, not
   an independent sensitivity, and the column labels themselves were accurate: the "stitched 2001–2024" values (e.g.
   Grasslands GPP–NIRv 0.686) really include 2022–2024. The month-by-month inputs of Figure 5 and Table S8 are identical
   (same land-type means, same units, same no-QA GNDVI; maximum difference 0).
2. **The "stitched 2001–2024" column was built on invalid MOD17 inputs.**
   - *Duplicated 2022 exports.* In every California-wide 2022 MOD17A2H export and in every land-type raster derived from it,
     9 of the 12 monthly files are byte-identical and two more form a second identical pair (`mod17_export_hashes_2022_2024.csv`);
     the small monthly differences in the land-type series come only from dividing the same total by the number of days.
     2023 and 2024 exports are all distinct. The 2022 GPP months are not observations.
   - *Two unconverted months.* The MOD17 unit conversion (kgC m⁻² month⁻¹ → gC m⁻² d⁻¹) was applied only when a raster's
     median was < 0.2 (`detrending_utils.py`). Natural Forests 2023-05 and 2023-06 exceeded that threshold and remained in
     kgC m⁻² month⁻¹ (0.236, 0.232 instead of 7.62, 7.75 gC m⁻² d⁻¹) (`mod17_months_left_unconverted.csv`).
   Spatial masks, averaging, YYYY-MM alignment and period selection were otherwise consistent; no column was reversed.
